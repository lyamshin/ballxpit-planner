BALL x PIT

Plan the run before you're in it

Every evolution needs specific level-3 balls — and building one spends them.

  1. Click what you're chasing. It waits as a ghost in your slots, and everything you still need turns amber.

  2. Shift-click what you get, as you get it. Land a ball you could build and its parts are consumed — same as the reactor.

  3. Fuse (X) pairs any two balls. If a pair evolves instead, it says so.
